Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your email address and user ID when you sign in via Google OAuth or email/password (managed via Supabase Authentication).
  • Audit & Model Data: Numeric embedding vectors, retention scores, timeline timestamps, and short hook transcripts generated when you audit a video or train a model.
  • Payment Metadata: Order IDs, purchased credit packs, and payout email addresses (PayPal/Wise) if you request creator royalty payouts.

2. How Your Videos Are Handled

When you audit an unreleased video, it is compressed locally on your device before transmission. Temporary video and audio files are deleted from our analysis servers immediately after processing completes. We do not publish or share your unreleased videos.

3. Payments

Credit purchases are processed securely by Gumroad. PULSE never collects or stores your credit card or banking numbers.

4. Third-Party Services

PULSE relies on Supabase (authentication and database), Gumroad (checkout and billing), and cloud GPU infrastructure solely to deliver the service. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Deletion & Contact

You can delete your trained models inside the app at any time. To request full account deletion or ask privacy questions, email shilali003@gmail.com.
